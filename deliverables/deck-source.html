The problem

Regulatory barrier dropped in 2017.
Economic barrier stayed.

The FIA lifted its single-seater ban on disabled drivers in December 2017. The barrier stopped being regulatory. It became economic. A paid race engineer is a luxury good for amateur + clubman series.

Three constituencies, one shared product gap: adaptive racers, veteran-team drivers, and grassroots clubman racers. Current AI race-engineer tools (Track Titan, Trophi.ai, Deep Dynamics) assume able-bodied physics. APEX is the first integrated workflow for adaptive hand-controls.

Beat 7 · Architectural killshot

COA-parameterized
brake-throttle
simultaneity gate.

The same coaching pipeline produces different physics corrections for adaptive versus able-bodied drivers, governed by the driver's FIA Certificate of Adaptations.

When the COA permits simultaneous brake + throttle (as adaptive racing programmes + adapted-hand-control systems commonly do), Stage 2's feasibility filter recognises it. When the COA does not permit it, the gate flags the input. To our literature review through 2026-Q2, the first public AI race-engineer workflow we found that reads the COA at the tensor level.
