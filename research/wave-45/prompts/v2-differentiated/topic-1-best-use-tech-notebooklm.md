## Prompt (paste verbatim once sources are uploaded)

You have the BeMyApp judging rubric, the IBM SkillsBuild Official Rules, and the APEX submission artifacts in your sources.

Then: write a one-paragraph "judge-tasting-note" assessment for each of the four BeMyApp prize categories (1st Place / Runner-up / Best Use of Technology / Most Innovative) explaining why APEX would or would not win that specific category.

Then: identify the single most load-bearing claim APEX makes that, if a brutal judge disputed it, would tank the submission. Cite the source passage that makes that claim.

Then: identify one specific edit Stephen could make to README + paper + Q&A flashcards before the 2026-05-31 submission deadline that would meaningfully raise the Best Use of Technology score.

Cite everything. No hallucination; only claim what your sources support.
